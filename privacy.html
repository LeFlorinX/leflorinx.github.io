Privacy Policy

About Terms of Service Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and a protected (hashed) password.
  • Your business details, brand notes, media files and captions.
  • For each connected social account: its public name, an account ID and the access tokens the platform gives us. Tokens are encrypted and stay on our server.
  • A history of scheduled posts and their results.
  • Payment status from Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

How we use it

Only to run EasyAdvert for you: to log you in, store your media, publish your posts at the times you chose, show your history, and handle your plan. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

  • TikTok (and later other platforms you connect): the videos and captions you schedule.
  • Stripe: what it needs to take payments.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. When you close your account or disconnect a platform, we stop using that data and delete or anonymise it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as invoices.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can disconnect any social account at any time from the Schedule page. Ask through your EasyAdvert account contact.

Security

Passwords are hashed, platform tokens are encrypted, and media files are shown only to you and the EasyAdvert administrator.
