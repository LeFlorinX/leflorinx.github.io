Your videos, posted on the days and hours you choose.

EasyAdvert helps small businesses keep their social media busy. Upload your own videos once, pick a schedule, and EasyAdvert publishes them to your TikTok account through TikTok's official API.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account. You approve access on TikTok's own page; EasyAdvert never sees your password.
  2. Upload videos you own and approve the ones that may be posted.
  3. Write a caption for each and add it to your queue.
  4. Choose days and hours. At each time, the next video in your queue is posted.
